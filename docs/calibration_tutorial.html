1 · PREPARE

Before you start

Do this once per session. Each item prevents a specific problem later.

  1. Robot on the stand, all four feet in the air. Body level. Legs must swing freely.
  2. Power off, move each joint once by hand through its range. Check that no cable catches.
  3. Wiring: ESP32 D21 → SDA, D22 → SCL, 3V3 → VCC, GND → GND. Separate 5 V (5 A or more) supply on the PCA9685 V+ screw terminal.
  4. Firmware uploaded (hardware/esp32_servo_bridge). In the Arduino Serial Monitor, ? gets READY.
  5. Close the Arduino Serial Monitor. Only one program can use the COM port.
  6. An angle meter: a phone inclinometer app, or a protractor.
  7. Install pyserial once: pip install pyserial
FIRST TIME?Practise the keys without hardware first. Nothing is sent anywhere:
python hardware/calibrate.py --dry-run

Then start for real, from the project folder (C:\Lecture\MyProject\Robot_dog). Use your own COM number, from Device Manager → Ports:

python hardware/calibrate.py --port COM5 ===== fl_1 (PCA9685 channel 0) ===== [joint 1 of 12 · 0/12 complete] front-left leg · roll: swings the whole leg sideways sim range: -6.0° to 18.0° measure: Stand IN FRONT of the robot. Hold the phone upright, flat against the outer face of the shoulder bracket. 0° = bracket vertical. pose 1 (key 1): phone reads 0.0° (bracket vertical, leg straight down) pose 2 (key 2): phone reads 15.0°, bottom of the bracket tilted OUTWARD = pose 1 turned 15° OUTWARD now 1500 µs | center=None dir=None us/rad=None min=None max=None NEXT: POSE 1. Get the phone to read 0.0° (bracket vertical, leg straight down). Press d (+10 µs) or a (-10 µs) and watch the number: if it moves away from the target, switch keys. Finish with c / z (±2 µs). Then press 1.
FOLLOW NEXTAfter every key, the line starting with NEXT: tells you the one thing to do next: what the phone should read, which way to turn, and, once pose 2 is done, exactly which key (a or d) moves the leg which way. The header shows which joint you're on and how many are complete. This page explains the why and the how.
HEADS UPThe moment a joint is selected, its servo jumps to 1500 µs (or to its saved center). Keep your hand near the leg the first time.

4 · THE ROUTINE

Seven steps, the same for every joint

Repeat this for all 12 joints. The program starts at fl_1; n moves to the next.

  1. Look at the joint header

    DoRead the joint name, then set the picker in section 3 to the same joint. Pose 1 and pose 2 are printed with their exact angles.
    SeeThe leg moves to 1500 µs (or to the saved center if this joint was done before).

  2. Reach pose 1, then press 1

    DoUse a/d until the angle meter is close to the pose 1 target, then z/c to get within ±0.5°. If the number moves away from the target, use the other key. Then press 1.
    Seecenter = 1480 µs saved. Your number will differ.
    CHECKIf center is far from 1500 (below ~1150 or above ~1850), there may not be room on both sides. Consider re-seating the horn now (section 6).

  3. Reach pose 2, then press 2

    DoTurn the leg to the pose 2 target: 30° past pose 1 (roll: 15°), in the direction the picker shows. Use a/d, then z/c. Then press 2.
    Seedirection=-1, us_per_rad=599.7 (10.47 µs/deg). Below it: pose 2 is ___ µs from pose 1. For 30° that is usually 293–388 µs (15° roll: 146–194 µs); these servos measured about 650–710 µs/rad.
    WHYTwo known angles and their pulses define a straight line. Direction is which way the servo turns; us_per_rad is how many µs per radian. Measure carefully here: an error of 1° makes every later angle about 3% off.

  4. Find one end, press 3

    DoMove slowly toward one end. Near the end switch to z/c. When the leg touches anything, back off 30–50 µs (3–5°) and press 3.
    STOPA continuous buzz means the servo is pushing against something. Move back right away or press r. A stalled MG90S gets hot within seconds.

  5. Find the other end, press 4

    DoSame at the opposite end. Which end is 3 and which is 4 doesn't matter.
    Seemin_us=964, max_us=1884 and then either sim range … fits inside the physical limits ✔ or a ⚠ warning (section 6).

  6. Save, then test with t

    DoPress s first. Then press t and watch: the leg goes standing → one sim limit → the other → standing. It takes 2–6 seconds.
    SeeSmooth movement, no buzzing, no touching, and it ends back in the standing pose. The status line ends with ✔ done.
    NOTEKeys don't work during the sweep. In an emergency, press Ctrl+C or switch off the servo supply. Saving before t means you lose nothing if you have to.

  7. Save again, tick it off, next joint

    DoPress s, tick the joint in section 7, then press n.
    SeeSaved: …\hardware_calibration.json, then the next joint's header.
TIPServos you already finished keep holding their last pulse while you work on the next joint. So when you calibrate a knee, its hip stays in the standing pose. That's why the order roll → hip → knee works well. After a restart, every servo starts limp again.
5 · SAVING

When your work is on disk, and when it isn't

Keys 1–4 only change values in memory. The file is written only by s and q.

SituationSaved?
You press s✔ yes
You press q✔ yes
You press n or pNo. Still only in memory. The program reminds you, and the header shows unsaved changes.
You close the terminal windowNo. Everything since the last s is lost.
The program stops with an error (USB unplugged, ESP32 error)No. Everything since the last s is lost.
RULEPress s twice per joint: before t and after t looks good. Always leave with q.

Stopping and coming back later

Saved joints are loaded automatically next time. Start at the first joint you haven't finished:

python hardware/calibrate.py --port COM5 --joint fr_1

A finished joint opens at its saved standing pulse, so you can check it with g. To redo a joint, just do 1–4 again and press s. The new values replace the old ones.

The file's "status" tells you where you are: PARTIAL_DO_NOT_RUN_POLICY until all 12 joints are complete, then MEASURED.

Re-seating a horn

  1. Press r so the servo goes limp.
  2. Remove the horn screw, pull the horn off, and put it back one tooth further in the direction that centers the range. Refit the screw.
  3. Old values for this joint are now wrong. Redo it from step 2 of the routine (1 → 2 → 3 → 4), then s.

Your 12 joints

Tick a joint only after t looked good and you pressed s. Ticks are kept in this browser only.

8 · WHEN ALL 12 ARE DONE

Finish and check

  1. Press q. Open hardware_calibration.json and check "status": "MEASURED".
  2. Sanity check the directions: left and right legs are mirrored, so a joint and its mirror (for example fl_2 and fr_2) usually have opposite direction.
  3. Keep a copy of the file. It belongs to these servos: if you swap a servo or move a horn, redo that joint.
  4. Next: send the standing pose to all 12 servos at once and compare with the simulator (bring-up step 4 in Servo Bench).
